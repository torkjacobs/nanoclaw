# Thread: Why Your AI Assistant Needs Governance Before It Needs Features

Hook style: Bold claim

---

🧵 1/6

Governance is item 38 on your AI backlog, somewhere between "dark mode" and "maybe add a mascot."

This is the wrong order. And the consequences are not theoretical.

---TWEET---

🧵 2/6

Samsung engineers pasted proprietary code into ChatGPT. Air Canada's AI invented a bereavement discount a tribunal forced them to honour. A law firm submitted fabricated case citations.

None of these teams were careless. They just deployed capable AI without governance.

---TWEET---

🧵 3/6

Three non-negotiable capabilities before you go live:

1. PII detection — in the request path, not a batch job overnight
2. Audit receipts — structured records, not log files
3. Policy enforcement — in code, not just system prompt suggestions

---TWEET---

🧵 4/6

Adding governance after launch isn't twice as hard. It's an order of magnitude harder.

Your database already has months of unscanned conversations with PII you can't identify. Your audit trail has gaps you can't fill. Your architecture wasn't designed for middleware.

---TWEET---

🧵 5/6

A system prompt that says "do not discuss competitors" is a request. A governance layer that scans output and blocks competitor mentions is enforcement.

The difference matters when your AI decides to be helpful and recommends a rival's product.
