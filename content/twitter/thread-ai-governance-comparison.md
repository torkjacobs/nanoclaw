# Thread: I Compared 5 AI Platforms on Governance

Hook style: Most people don't know

---

🧵 1/7

Most people don't know this: the majority of AI customer platforms have zero PII detection.

I tested 5 platforms — Tidio, Chatbase, Intercom Fin, Freshchat, and Tork Chat — on one dimension only: governance.

Not features. Not pricing. Governance. Here's what I found.

---TWEET---

🧵 2/7

I tested 6 capabilities:

1. PII detection
2. Audit trail
3. Compliance receipts
4. Escalation controls
5. Data isolation
6. Policy enforcement

These aren't nice-to-haves. They're the baseline for any AI system handling customer data in a regulated environment.

---TWEET---

🧵 3/7

Tidio and Chatbase: no PII detection. Customer credit card numbers sit in conversation logs. Escalation is manual or keyword-based. Policy enforcement relies on training data or system prompts.

Fast to deploy. But if you have compliance obligations, you're exposed on day one.

---TWEET---

🧵 4/7

Intercom Fin is the most mature. Content filtering, detailed logs, rule-based escalation, workspace data isolation.

But no cryptographic compliance receipts. And the governance features come bundled with enterprise pricing and the full Intercom platform.

---TWEET---

🧵 5/7

Freshchat has basic sentiment detection — an edge over most. But no compliance receipts, no runtime output scanning, no signed audit records.

Adequate for low-regulation environments. Not enough for industries where a regulator might ask what your AI said on Tuesday at 14:32.

---TWEET---

🧵 6/7

The test that matters: send your AI a message with a test credit card number. Check your conversation logs, your LLM provider's API logs, and your database.

If the number appears in any of them — you don't have PII detection. You have a liability.
