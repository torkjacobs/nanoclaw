14 days. That's how long it took to deploy a fully governed AI-powered customer experience for a vehicle rental company.

Not a proof of concept. Not a demo. A production system handling real customer conversations with PII detection, compliance monitoring, and human escalation built in from day one.

Here's what the timeline actually looked like:

Week 1:
→ Knowledge base ingestion (pricing, policies, fleet data)
→ Multi-agent pipeline configured (routing, retrieval, governance)
→ PII detection tuned for the automotive rental context
→ Widget deployed on their existing site

Week 2:
→ Staff training on the escalation dashboard
→ Edge case testing (damage claims, insurance questions, complaints)
→ Compliance review with their legal team
→ Go-live with real customer traffic

The reason this was possible in 2 weeks isn't magic. It's architecture.

When governance, PII detection (~20ms), and escalation are part of the platform — not custom code you write per deployment — the integration work shrinks dramatically. 124 adapters and 11 SDKs handle most of the plumbing.
