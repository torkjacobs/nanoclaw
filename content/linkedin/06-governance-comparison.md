I tested 5 AI platforms on one question: "What happens when your AI says something it shouldn't?"

The answers were... revealing.

Most platforms treat governance as a settings page. A toggle for "content moderation." Maybe a profanity filter. That's it.

Here's what I evaluated across all 5:

→ PII detection — Is it real-time? Does it catch context-dependent PII (case numbers, account refs)?
→ Compliance monitoring — Can you set custom rules per industry?
→ Escalation logic — Does the system know when it's out of its depth?
→ Audit trails — Can you reconstruct why any response was given?
→ Brand safety — Does it prevent off-topic or off-brand responses?

The results split into two clear camps:

Camp 1: "We have content moderation" (basic keyword filtering, no real-time PII detection, no audit trail)

Camp 2: "Governance is a runtime layer" (sub-20ms PII scanning, per-tenant compliance rules, full response tracing)

The gap isn't incremental. It's architectural. You can't retrofit real governance onto a platform that treats it as an afterthought.
