AI governance won't prevent every mistake. And that's exactly the point.

There's a dangerous narrative in the AI space right now: that with enough guardrails, your AI will never hallucinate, never offend, never make an error.

That's not how any safety system works.

Seatbelts don't prevent car accidents. They reduce harm when accidents happen. Governance for AI works the same way.

Here's what honest AI governance actually does:

→ It catches PII before it reaches the model (~20ms detection)
→ It flags responses that violate compliance rules before they reach customers
→ It escalates to humans when confidence drops below threshold
→ It creates audit trails so you can investigate when things go wrong

Notice what's missing: a guarantee of perfection.

The companies I trust most with AI aren't the ones claiming zero error rates. They're the ones who can tell you exactly what happens when an error occurs — how fast it's caught, who's notified, and how the system adapts.

That's the standard we hold ourselves to at Tork. Not invincibility. Preparedness.
